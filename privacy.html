Privacy Policy

FragFable Poster · Effective 25 November 2026

What FragFable Poster is

FragFable Poster is a private, self-hosted tool run by FragFable TV. Its only user is the owner of the FragFable TV channels, who uses it to upload short video clips to their own YouTube channel and their own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

FragFable Poster uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By connecting a YouTube account to it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload a video, with its title, description, privacy setting and thumbnail, to the connected channel.
  • Read-only access to the connected channel (youtube.readonly): only to show the name of the channel that is connected.
  • Instagram (through Meta's Instagram API): to publish a Reel to the owner's own Instagram account. Meta's handling of data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

It does not read, collect or store data about any other user, channel, viewer or comment.

What it stores, and where

  • The sign-in token that lets it upload to the connected YouTube channel, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • For each upload: the video's ID, link, title and the time it was posted, stored only on the owner's own computer, so the same clip is not uploaded twice.

Nothing is stored on any other server. FragFable Poster uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

The sign-in token is kept until the account is disconnected in the tool's Settings, which deletes it and revokes it with Google. Access can also be removed at any time from Google's security settings (third-party access). Upload records stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
